**Q: Do I need to change my existing projects?**
A: No. Existing projects continue to work unchanged. Enhancements are backward compatible.

**Q: Will the AI behave differently?**
A: AI behavior is enhanced but not fundamentally changed. You may notice better reasoning and communication.

**Q: Are the templates different?**
A: Templates are enhanced but maintain the same structure. Existing templates continue to work.

**Q: How do I access the new features?**
A: Most features are automatic. For optional enhancements, reference the new templates and guidelines.

**Q: What if I prefer the old way?**
A: You can continue using YASK exactly as before. All original functionality is preserved.
